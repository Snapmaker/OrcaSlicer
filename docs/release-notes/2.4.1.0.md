# EdgeSlicer 2.4.1.0

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.1.0: a rebuilt Bambu Lab Device tab with a two-sided AMS layout, AMS drying, both nozzle temperatures and printer storage browsing. Timelapses now record on the H2 series, and idle nozzles cool down on dual-nozzle printers.
Seams: Aligned left/right, and aligned seams now hide in the joints of multi-part models.
Fixes:
- H2D/H2C print-by-object jobs no longer freeze when switching back to a nozzle.
- The Bambu Device page no longer disconnects and reconnects every minute.
- Snapmaker U1 printers no longer show as offline in the phone app.
- Dark mode text backgrounds and several crashes are fixed.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.0.1](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.0.1-edge) is included.

## What's new

### Bambu Lab Device tab

- **Dual-extruder AMS layout.** On the H2D, H2D Pro, H2C and X2D, each nozzle's AMS units are shown on their own side, with humidity, slot tiles and feed lines to that nozzle. The loaded path is drawn in the filament's colour.
- **AMS drying.** The humidity readout opens an AMS Dryness Control dialog on the AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT. It shows status, temperature and time remaining, and uses Bambu's temperature and time limits.
- **Both nozzle temperatures.** Two-nozzle printers show L and R temperature rows, each with its own editable target.
- **Printer storage browser.** Timelapse videos and model files on the printer can be listed, downloaded, deleted and printed straight from the printer, over the local network.
- **Bambu green** buttons and progress bar on this page, and the guide/retry/tips column is removed.

### Printing on Bambu Lab printers

- **Timelapse** now records on the H2D, H2C, H2D Pro, H2S, P2S and X2D. The per-layer photo command was missing from their G-code.
- **Idle nozzle cool-down** on dual-nozzle printers (H2D, H2D Pro, H2C, X2D). The nozzle that isn't printing cools down and is heated back up just before it's needed, as in Bambu Studio.
- **Filament arrangement before slicing** on two-nozzle printers. You confirm or sync the filament-to-nozzle arrangement with the printer before slicing, and Send is blocked when the printer refuses the nozzle mapping.
- **Prime tower:** rib walls on Bambu Lab printers, and separate "tower interface" options.
- **Export and open in Bambu Studio** from the File menu.

### Seams

- **Aligned left and Aligned right** replace Left and Right. They pick the best-hidden spot on each layer, biased toward that side, the way Aligned back favours the back.
- **Hide seam in part joints** (on by default). All aligned seam modes place the seam where two parts of a model touch, including touching objects on the plate. Single-part models slice exactly as before.

### Multi-colour painting

- **Top and bottom paint penetration layers** set how many layers deep painted colour goes, independently of the shell count. They use Bambu Studio's settings, and 0 keeps today's behaviour.

### Phone app and hub

- **Native device controls** in the companion app: status, camera, temperatures, AMS, speed, light, fans, filament load/unload and printer error actions. Tapping a notification opens that printer.
- **Reprint** covers every printer type, not just Bambu. Jobs are grouped by printer model, and Snapmaker U1 upload-only jobs can be started in place.
- **Duplicate notifications and history entries** are fixed.
- **Snapmaker U1 status** is read from the printer's local Moonraker interface, so U1s no longer flip to offline.
- **Switch to remote** now stays on the remote address.

### macOS and Linux

- **Bambu Lab printers on macOS and Linux.** The EdgeSlicer network plug-in (UltraNet) is now bundled with the macOS and Linux builds. It covers local-network discovery, status, sending and printing. This is its first release on these platforms: please report problems. Camera live view isn't available there yet.

## Fixes

- **H2D/H2C print-by-object.** Jobs no longer freeze when returning to a nozzle. The idle nozzle is no longer reheated between objects.
- **H2C Print on a sliced plate** no longer resets the slice. **H2D sends** are no longer refused by the nozzle-mapping check.
- **Bambu Device page reconnect loop.** Each EdgeSlicer window now uses its own printer connection, so windows no longer knock each other off. A quiet printer is asked for its status instead of being disconnected.
- **Loading filament from a third AMS or an AMS HT** addressed the wrong slot. It now matches Bambu Studio.
- **Dark mode:** mismatched grey boxes behind text on the Device tab, in Preferences and in other dialogs are gone.
- **Crashes fixed:**
  - Ctrl-clicking another object while a painting tool is open;
  - several crashes while closing EdgeSlicer;
  - start-up when the settings file doesn't end with a new line;
  - several Linux crashes (web views, the slice-mode menu, a missing SSL certificate setting).
- **Print-by-object notices** are warnings, with "Do not show again".
- **Snapmaker account** signs in silently at start-up.
- **Filament sync** no longer drops all filaments when the first toolhead is empty.
- **Security:** post-processing scripts in 3MF files from others aren't run, links are checked, and the local page server is locked down further.
- **Support filament matching** now follows painted colours and brims.
