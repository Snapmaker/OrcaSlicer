# EdgeSlicer 2.4.4.2

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.4.2:
- Snapmaker U1: 54 Polymaker and Panchroma filament presets.
- New printer profiles: Prusa CORE One INDX 4T/8T, Creality CR-10S Pro and SPARKX i8, Anycubic Kobra 3 V2, Kobra 3 Max V2 and Kobra 4.
- Profiles of 49 vendors refreshed to current OrcaSlicer, and default presets repaired on about 200 printers (some defaults change).
- G-code export and slicing speed-ups ported from OrcaSlicer.

Fixes:
- Printers you untick in Printer Selection stay removed.
- Text tool: font search finds fonts again.
- macOS: one Dock icon, the window no longer hides, the 3D toolbar has its normal size.
- Layer cooling counts G4 P dwells; cut retraction accepts 0-100 mm (0 = off).
- Kobra 3, Bambu A2L, Qidi Q1 Pro and toolchanger profiles no longer fail or crash.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.4.1](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.4.1-edge) is included.

## New

- **Polymaker and Panchroma filaments for the Snapmaker U1** (#337). 54 presets for the U1 (0.4 nozzle): Panchroma (CoPE, PLA, Celestial, Galaxy, Glow, Luminous, Marble, Matte, Metallic, Neon, Satin, Silk, Starlight, Translucent, UV Shift), PolyLite, PolyTerra, Polymaker and Fiberon materials. 50 come from Polymaker's own published U1 presets (MIT licence, notice shipped with the profiles); 4 are derived (Panchroma PLA Temp Shift, PolyLite ABS, PolyLite ASA, Polymaker ASA) from Polymaker's Galaxy and H2C presets. Each is a thin override of the matching Generic U1 preset and carries only material properties (temperatures, fans, cooling, density, cost, flow). Pressure advance stays off, as on the other U1 presets. Start/end G-code, retraction and tool-change values are not imported.
- **Prusa CORE One INDX 4T and 8T** (#340). The two toolchangers, ported from OrcaSlicer: machines, 4 process presets (0.10 to 0.25 mm) and 6 INDX filaments. This needed a new filament setting, **Chamber minimal temperature** (Target / Minimal row on the filament page; the INDX start G-code reads it). EdgeSlicer no longer adds its own chamber heat-up command before a start G-code that already sets the chamber temperature, and the Standard / High Flow selectors no longer appear on a printer whose only variant is High Flow. Slices of both machines with one and four filaments were checked from the command line. Not tested on a printer.
- **Creality profiles** (#312, #314). CR-10S Pro V2 (from OrcaSlicer), CR-10S Pro (same preset under its own name) and SPARKX i8 (from Creality Print 7.3), each with six process presets (0.08 to 0.28 mm) and matching filaments. Settings this build does not define were dropped from the i8 files, and a private LAN address in them was removed. The i8 is not tested on a printer. The Creality K3 is not included.
- **Anycubic profiles** (#313, #317). Kobra 3 V2 (from OrcaSlicer), Kobra 3 Max V2 and Kobra 4 (from Anycubic Slicer Next 2.0.0.3, values taken as Anycubic ships them). All slice from the command line. The Kobra 3 Max V2 and Kobra 4 are not tested on a printer.
- **Qidi and Anycubic refresh** (#315). The existing Qidi (02.04.00.17) and Anycubic presets are brought up to current OrcaSlicer: new filament ids, normalised machine limit arrays, obsolete keys removed, refreshed artwork and bed models, the new Qidi X-Plus 5 chamber settings. Preset names stay as they were, so user presets that inherit from them keep working.
- **Vendor profile refresh** (#320). 49 vendors that were behind OrcaSlicer main are refreshed (for example Prusa, Sovol, Ratrig, Z-Bolt, Tiertime, Volumic, Voron, Artillery, Eryone, FLSun, Wanhao France, and OrcaFilamentLibrary with 677 new filaments). Our own preset names, bed artwork and local fixes are kept, and nothing is renamed or removed. Not refreshed: Elegoo and Custom. See Behaviour changes.
- **Hub web pages: accessibility** (#328). On the phone hub's Stream, Devices and hub pages, selected states no longer rely on colour alone (ticks, bold and underline, glyphs for errors and warnings), muted text and green buttons meet a 4.5:1 contrast, high-contrast (`prefers-contrast: more`) and reduced-motion settings are honoured, camera chips, tabs, dialog buttons and device buttons work from the keyboard and have proper names, and the QR codes have text alternatives. HTML only; the Slice tab was left alone. Not checked with a screen reader here.
- **Slicing speed-ups ported from OrcaSlicer** (#304, #305, #306, #307, adapted to EdgeSlicer's own G-code code). Klipper acceleration commands skip work when nothing changes, adaptive pressure advance is skipped when no used filament enables it, and G-code text, number formatting and the G-code reader allocate less (#304, #305). The overhang and curled-wall estimates run in parallel before G-code generation (#306), and the curled-wall estimate is skipped when overhang speed or curled-perimeter slowdown is off (#307). OrcaSlicer reports up to about 4 to 17 percent faster export on overhang-heavy models and about 9 percent faster slicing with curled slowdown off; we have not benchmarked EdgeSlicer. These changes are not meant to alter G-code; the byte-for-byte comparisons listed in the PRs were still to be run when they merged.

## Behaviour changes

- **Vendor profiles refreshed** (#320, #315). The refreshed vendors take OrcaSlicer's current values for the presets it changed, so default speeds, temperatures, filament ids, machine limits and bed shapes of some presets differ from before. Presets with a local EdgeSlicer edit were kept. Cubicon exhaust fan speeds went from 255 to 100 (the allowed range is 0 to 100). User presets and projects that inherit by name still resolve, but a project that relied on an old default value can slice slightly differently.
- **Default presets repaired** (#319, #321, #324, #325, #326, #327, #338). Many machines named a default process or filament that did not exist, was not compatible, or could not slice (for example a 0.20 mm layer on a 0.2 mm nozzle). About 200 machines now default to an existing compatible preset: 145 in 27 vendors (Ratrig V-Core 4, Volumic, Anker M5, Artillery, Tiertime and others), 18 Creality machines, Anycubic Kobra X, Qidi Q2 and Q2C, 23 Flashforge machines and the Snapmaker A250/A350 0.8 nozzle. Creality 0.2 mm nozzles default to 0.16 mm Optimal, the K2 Plus and K2 Pro 0.2 max layer height is 0.14 mm, and three Ender-3 0.2 mm Standard processes use 0.25 mm widths where 0.2 mm was refused. Anycubic Kobra Max, Kobra Plus and Vyper got the missing `G92 E0` in the layer-change G-code, and Dremel 3D40/3D45, Raise3D Pro3 Plus and Wanhao D12-300 got their missing extruder reset. A new project on one of these machines can show a different default process than before.
- **Cut retraction range** (#322). Retraction distance when cut is now 0 to 100 mm (was 10 to 18 mm), and 0 means no cut retraction: no `M620.11 ... E-0` is emitted, even when the long-retraction options are on. Bambu presets (10 to 18 mm) emit the same G-code as before. The suggested minimum flush volume is clamped at 0.
- **Layer cooling** (#333). `G4 P` dwells (milliseconds) were read as 0 s, so only `G4 S` dwells counted towards layer time. Both now count, so layers that contain such a dwell (for example in a filament-change G-code) are slowed down less. Measured on a Creality SPARKX i7 with its shipped 8 s threshold, no layer changed; with the threshold forced to 60 s, layers 2 to 30 sped up by about 2 to 15 percent (one short layer by 67 percent).
- **Internal bridges** (#308). The expansion of the anchoring boundary of internal bridges over sparse infill was in the wrong unit and had no effect; it is now 30 percent of the line spacing. G-code changes only on layers with internal bridges over sparse infill, where bridge lines now reach their anchor edge more reliably.
- **Bed exclusion area** (#323). `bed_exclude_area` was read in two different ways in different parts of the program (one polygon in some, groups of 4 points in others). It is now read one way: if every group of 4 points is a rectangle it is a list of rectangles, otherwise one polygon (such as a ring). Every shipped list that worked gives the same result as before. The Anycubic Kobra 3 ring, which made arrange find no room and the command line fail with "no object fully inside", now works, and the Kobra 3 profile ships an empty list (#315).
- **Machines with more than two extruders** (#329) that list several nozzle variants per extruder are now treated as ordinary toolchangers (filament n on tool n) instead of Bambu-style dual-nozzle grouping. No shipped profile changes.
- **Shared config files** (#345). The installed-printer list in `EdgeSlicer.conf` is now merged per printer rather than unioned, so a printer removed in one instance is not re-added by an older copy in another (see Fixes).

## Fixes

- **Printers unticked in Printer Selection came back** (#345). Unticking a printer in "Select/Remove printers (system presets)" removed it from the combo, but the next idle save merged the old list from disk back in, so reopening the dialog showed it ticked again. The list is now merged per printer against what this instance last loaded or saved: removals stick, additions from another instance survive, and a stale instance (such as the phone hub) no longer re-adds a removed printer. Recent projects still use the old union.
- **Text tool font search** (#344). Typing in the font dropdown blanked the whole list on every run after the first, because the cached font list restored the fonts but not their names. Search now works from the cache, ignores case, keeps matches at the same position in alphabetical order and restores the full list when cleared. Removing an unloadable font no longer erases from an empty name list (a crash).
- **macOS Dock tile, window and toolbar** (#339, tested by hand on a Mac). The Dock tile no longer shows the unpadded Windows art. The phone hub's extra Dock tile, which brought a window-less process forward and left the slicer behind other apps, is gone (only the menu-bar icon remains). The 3D toolbar is no longer tiny on Retina displays: the stored scale was compared with the framebuffer scale instead of the logical one (the log notes a change as `3D toolbar auto size`).
- **Settings that could not be turned off** (#303). A text value passed to the config's bool setter was stored as `true`. The measure gizmo options (curve smooth shell, exact highlight), several orient and arrange options (rotation, minimum area, align, avoid the calibration area) and the code-emboss decorate and light-part toggles therefore always read as on. They now keep `0` and `1`. Reading a boolean from one section no longer also looks at the app section. The wizard-finished flag still stores `true`.
- **First slice showed no progress** (#301, from OrcaSlicer). If the progress notification had been dropped, the first slice after launch started without a progress state and showed nothing. The popup also stays anchored when Daily Tips collapse or expand.
- **Preview on Linux Flatpak** (#300, from OrcaSlicer). Shell and toolpath buffers were created or deleted in whichever OpenGL context was current, which on Flatpak can belong to the web view. They now use the shown canvas. Hand testing on a Flatpak build was still open when it merged.
- **Bambu A2L failed to slice** (#331). The A2L layer-change G-code uses `curr_y_acceleration_limit`, which EdgeSlicer did not define (command line error -100). The bed-slinger mass model of Bambu Studio is ported: the `machine_max_force_Y` and `machine_bed_mass_Y` printer settings (A2L: 29 N, 2700 g) and the `curr_y_acceleration_limit`, `curr_accumulated_mass` and `curr_layer_mass` variables. A cube on the A2L emits `M201 N1 Y8000` each layer; heavier prints lower it. Other printers produce the same G-code (the two settings stay out of the config block while both are 0).
- **Toolchanger profiles ran out of memory or crashed** (#329). Klipper toolchanger presets in the per-variant layout (upstream's MyToolChanger 0.2 to 0.8: five extruders with three variants each) were taken for Bambu-style dual-nozzle machines. The nozzle grouping then read past the end of per-extruder arrays and used 4 to 20 GB or crashed. Grouping now applies only to one- or two-extruder machines, and its arrays are bounds-checked.
- **"Too small line width" on 0.2 mm nozzles** (#330). Skin and skeleton infill widths default to 100 percent of the nozzle, which on a 0.2 mm nozzle at 0.20 mm layers is not above the layer height, and were checked even when Locked Zag infill is not used. They are now checked only for regions that use Locked Zag, so such presets slice. No slicing code changed.
- **Qidi Q1 Pro prime tower in the command line** (#332). A default-placed prime tower touched the bed exclusion notches on the back edge (error -64 on all four nozzles). The command line now moves it to the first clear spot (the GUI's default position, then a bed corner), as the GUI already does; a tower that does not touch the area stays where it is. The GUI is unchanged.
- **Anycubic Kobra 3** (#323, #315): see Behaviour changes (bed exclusion area).
- The wizard loop, the FlashForge Creator 5 crash, macOS see-through objects and app icon, and the Linux build fix are in 2.4.4.1 and included.

## Profiles

- **Added:** Snapmaker U1 Polymaker and Panchroma filaments (#337); Prusa CORE One INDX 4T/8T (#340); Creality CR-10S Pro V2, CR-10S Pro and SPARKX i8 (#312, #314); Anycubic Kobra 3 V2, Kobra 3 Max V2 and Kobra 4 (#313, #317). Machines, process presets, filaments, bed models and cover images are included. INDX, SPARKX i8, Kobra 3 Max V2 and Kobra 4 were not tested on printers.
- **Refreshed:** 49 vendors (#320), Qidi and Anycubic (#315), plus a Creality cross-check against Creality Print 7.3 (#314). Vendor `.json` versions are bumped so installed copies update.
- **Sanity fixes** (#319, #321, #324, #325, #326, #327, #338, plus fixes inside #320). Each was found by slicing a centred 30 mm cube on every machine preset from the command line. Results from the PRs: Creality machines slicing by default 80 to 92 of 97, Anycubic and Qidi 63 to 75 of 79, Flashforge and Snapmaker 75 to 118 of 118, and 678 to 823 of 943 machines overall after #326. Most of what still fails by default are Prusa CORE One and MK4S machines whose processes only carry a `compatible_printers_condition` (accepted by the GUI, refused by the command-line check).
- **Not imported:** the Creality K3 and its multi-nozzle keys, Wanhao's 12 new D9 models (no cover images), 10 new upstream vendors, upstream's preset renames (our names are kept and references rewritten), and the Elegoo and Custom refreshes.
- **Changes from upstream** where this build differs: settings it does not define are dropped from imported Creality and Anycubic files, the Prusa INDX Generic ABS `filament_shrinkage_compensation_z` is 100% instead of the out-of-range 0.22%, and Anycubic machines keep `retraction_distances_when_cut` 18 where upstream has 0.

## Under the hood

- **Config merge** (#345). `AppConfig::merge_vendor_maps` is a three-way merge of (vendor, model, variant) against `m_vendors_on_disk`, and the printer-selection guide page keys the selection by vendor and model.
- **Bed exclusion helpers** (#323). `bed_exclude_area_is_rectangles`, `bed_exclude_area_polygons` and `bed_exclude_area_boxes` in PrintConfig replace the separate readers, and the print speed table no longer indexes an empty difference.
- **Cut retraction and cooling** (#322, #333). `long_retraction_when_cut_active()` feeds every consumer of the cut-retraction options. `CoolingBuffer` parses `G4` against `npos`, S as seconds and P as milliseconds, and ignores text after a `;` comment.
- **Wipe tower** (#332). `wipe_tower_position_clear_of_exclusion()` in `WipeTowerEstimate`, called from the command line's pre-slice clamp only.
- **Tests** merged with their PRs: bed exclusion shipped-list scan and ring/strip cases (#323, with #343 relaxing the shipped-list check after the Kobra 3 list became empty), cut retraction range (#322), cooling dwells (#333), extruder-variant arrays (#329), Locked Zag width validation (#330), mass-limited acceleration (#331), wipe tower clearance (#332), chamber temperature and High Flow detection (#340), AppConfig literals and vendor-map merge (#303, #345), font search (#344), the speed-up ports (#304 to #307) and macOS startup and toolbar logic (#339).
- **Release notes for 2.4.4.1** were merged to main (#342).
