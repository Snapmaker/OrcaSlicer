# EdgeSlicer 2.4.1.1

<!-- update-notice -->
2.4.1.1 fixes the seam position list: picking Aligned left, Aligned right or Random saved a different seam. Please re-check the seam position of any profile you changed in 2.4.1.0.
Also new: Auto-paint seam in the seam painting tool, an Aligned front seam position, and a Stable/Nightly update channel.
Fixes:
- Locked Zag skin/skeleton patterns saved a different pattern than the one picked, and three of them crashed the slice.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.1.0](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.1.0-edge) is included.

## Fixes

- **Seam position list.** Since Left/Right were added, the list's order didn't match the stored values. Picking **Aligned left** saved Random, **Aligned right** saved Left, and **Random** saved Right. Profiles that show one of those three may hold a different seam than intended: re-pick it once. Back, Aligned, Aligned back and Nearest were never affected. A new test now checks the order of every drop-down setting.
- **Locked Zag skin and skeleton patterns** (Strength, when sparse infill is Locked Zag). Every pick saved a different pattern: for example, Rectilinear saved Monotonic Line. "3D Honeycomb", "Lateral Lattice" and "Cross Hatch" saved patterns that crashed the slice, and "Grid" fell back to default. The menus now store what you pick, and a pattern Locked Zag can't use falls back to default instead of crashing. Existing profiles are not changed: if you use Locked Zag, re-pick both patterns once.

## New

- **Auto-paint seam.** The seam painting tool gets an Auto-paint section. It computes the seam exactly as the slicer would for Aligned, Aligned back/front/left/right, with or without "Hide seam in part joints", and paints it as an editable enforcer strip. It is one undo step.
- **Aligned front** seam position (the seam is biased toward the front). It is exported to Bambu Studio as "aligned".
- **Update channel.** Preferences › General › Update channel: **Stable** (default) or **Nightly**. Nightly builds are published as the `nightly` pre-release and are untested; see [docs/nightly.md](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/blob/main/docs/nightly.md).
- **Privacy:** [docs/privacy.md](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/blob/main/docs/privacy.md) now covers the phone app's push notification service: what it sees and keeps, and how to opt out.
