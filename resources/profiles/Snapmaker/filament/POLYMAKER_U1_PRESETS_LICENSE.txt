The Polymaker filament presets for the Snapmaker U1 bundled in this fork
("... @U1" files with filament_vendor "Polymaker") take their material values
from https://github.com/Polymaker3D/Polymaker-Preset (presets.polymaker.com,
preset/<Filament>/Snapmaker/U1/OrcaSlicer/, commit 31cec5acaf67) under the
following MIT License. Only material properties were imported (temperatures,
bed temperatures, cooling, density, cost, flow, volumetric speed); the files
were rewritten as thin overrides of the Snapmaker "Generic ... @U1 0.4 nozzle"
presets so the U1 tool-change and G-code settings stay Snapmaker's.
