# EdgeSlicer 2.4.2.0

<!-- update-notice -->
2.4.2.0 is a big feature release: a new Home tab, UI themes, Precise Seam, STEP export, QR code and shape embossing, a Blender bridge, and new placement tools.
New:
- Home tab with Recent projects, a model Library, Print History and vendor catalogues.
- UI themes (Ember Forge, Silver Bastion) in main menu › Themes.
- Precise Seam helper volumes, STEP export, QR/barcode/shape embossing, Edit in Blender.
- Exact fillet and chamfer on CAD parts (experimental).
- FLSun S1 Pro printer profile.
- Move › Snap face to surface, Lay on face for single parts, Assemble › Exact highlight.
- Process settings carry over when you switch printers.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.1.2](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.1.2-edge) is included.

## New

- **Home tab** (#208). It has four sections:
  - **Recent** projects.
  - A **Library** of your model folders, with search, filters and previews.
  - **Print History** from the G-code archive.
  - **Vendors**, which lists the models you have access to through a vendor's API (you set up the connector; credentials are kept in the system credential store).
- **UI themes** (#213, #216). Theme packs change colours, fonts, shapes, the title bar and the 3D view. Two ship with EdgeSlicer, Ember Forge and Silver Bastion, and you can make your own.
  - Open them from main menu › **Themes...**.
  - Colours apply straight away. Font changes offer a restart.
- **Precise Seam** (#201, #204, #206). Helper volumes that force, block or bias the seam where they touch the part: Center, Left, Right, Blocked, Enforced and Neutral. Without helpers, slicing output is unchanged.
- **STEP export** (#163). File › Export › **Export as STEP...** writes the selection or the whole plate. Parts imported from STEP keep their exact faces. The command line takes `--export-step` and now also accepts .step/.stp input.
- **Emboss QR codes, barcodes and shapes** (#205):
  - QR codes and barcodes (Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 39), with an optional logo and a square, rounded or circular surround.
  - Simple shapes: circle, square, polygons, star and ring.
- **Exact fillet and chamfer on CAD parts** (#218, experimental). Right-click a part › **Fillet / chamfer (CAD)...** to round or bevel picked edges with real curved faces. The exact shape is kept through save, reopen and STEP export. STL parts can be converted first. Shell (hollowing) is included but is slow and often fails on rounded parts; large fillets can fail on complex geometry, and the panel suggests the largest radius that works.
- **Blender bridge** (#212). Right-click a part › **Edit in Blender**. Saving in Blender updates the part in place. From Blender, **Send to EdgeSlicer** adds objects to the open window.
- **Move › Snap face to surface** (#211). Drag a face of the selection flush onto another object's surface, and spin it in fine or coarse steps. An optional target surface keeps it sliding on one face; hold Alt to release it.
- **Lay on face for single parts** (#214). The part turns so the picked face points down, then drops so that face is on the bed.
- **Assemble › Exact highlight** (#215). Shows exactly where a large face will land on a small target.
- **Process settings carry across printer switches** (#176). Your settings stay as revertable changes on the matched profile. On a nozzle-size change, line widths and layer heights follow the new nozzle.
- **FLSun S1 Pro** printer profile, with its own process presets (0.12–0.30 mm) and 14 filament presets at FLSun's S1 Pro speeds (#228). The S1's 0.24 mm Draft now really prints at 0.24 mm.
- **Top and bottom surface density** in the Strength tab (#188). Also: G-code editor placeholder `curr_bed_type` (#196), and a CLI fill for keys missing from a project (#182).

## Phone app and hub

- AMS and spools for every Bambu printer on the phone's printer screen, not only the selected one. This needs the updated network plug-in, which is included (#209).
- Cameras for Bambu printers are found on the phone (#190), and the Stream tab's camera list no longer loses cameras when several windows are open (#210).

## Fixes

- **Bambu sends:** a failed send explains why (no SD card, IP, access code, network, firmware lock) instead of a bare -3120 (#191). A printer's old errors no longer pop up as if it refused something EdgeSlicer sent (#192).
- **Filaments:**
  - New printer variants of filaments you already ticked show up automatically (#187).
  - The filament dialog only saves the vendor you ticked (#186).
  - The filament editor no longer carries unsaved edits into another slot (#164).
- **G-code and slicing:**
  - Overhang slowdown and fan apply only near the overhang (#181).
  - Toolchange G-code is no longer split by the fan mover (#170).
  - The print-time estimate comes after the config block (#166).
  - Arachne inner-outer-inner wall order on narrow walls (#183).
  - Deterministic painted multi-material slicing (#167).
  - The prime tower shows in Prepare when a filament is used only on outer walls (#193).
  - By-object printing with a mixed filament on walls no longer crashes on export (#202). Mixed plates now take temperatures and the prime tower from the mix's real filaments, and incompatible mixes (for example PLA+TPU) are blocked.
  - Painted seams whose patch wraps the start of the loop (#198), and seams on mirrored objects (#203).
- **3MF files:** plate ids and paint data are hardened (#185), and object/part settings are XML-escaped (#199).
- **Graphics and views:**
  - Plate thumbnails render reliably (#168).
  - Dark-mode plate icons (#179).
  - Ctrl+A selects oversized objects (#165).
  - The filament grouping dialog on re-slice (#180).
  - Local pages load from paths with spaces or # (#200).
- **Imports and platforms:** hardened OBJ import (#197), and Linux/Flatpak build fixes (#194, #195).
