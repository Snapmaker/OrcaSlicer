# EdgeSlicer 2.4.4.0

<!-- update-notice -->
New in 2.4.4.0:
- Curved text: bend text into an arch or smile, as Bent or Rigid letters, and place it letter by letter on curved surfaces with Use surface.
- Shapes, symbols and your own SVGs can now sit inside text, from the new Insert popup.
- Per-plate print history: a history icon on each plate shows when and where it was sent.
- Privacy: EdgeSlicer no longer contacts Snapmaker or Bambu at startup unless you use them and more printer/computer related data has been removed from the send process.
- H2D, H2C and X2D: proper shading and handling of exclusion zones and plate displays.
- FlashForge: Device tab lists your FlashForge printers and has an Add printer button.
- Phone app: creating a new pairing link now signs out all previously paired phones.

Fixes:
- The prime tower no longer ends up on the wrong plate after undo or redo in some specific scenarios.
- Small binary STL files (a 10 mm cube, for example) now load.
- The per-plate dual-nozzle confirmation is kept when a project is reopened.
- Check Windows Firewall now explains what to do on a Public network, and covers FlashForge discovery.
<!-- /update-notice -->

Everything in [2.4.3.0](https://github.com/aceRage/EdgeSlicer/releases/tag/v2.4.3.0-edge) is included.

## New

- **Curved text** (#287, #289). The Text tool has a new **Curve** section under Advanced.
  - **Curve text** bends the text along an arc. **Define by** Angle (span of the arc) or Radius, with a slider and number box; angle 0 gives straight text. A readout shows the resulting radius or span and warns when the curve had to be limited.
  - **Side:** Arch or Smile. **Letters:** Bent (the letter shapes follow the curve) or Rigid (each letter keeps its outline and is rotated into place). Spacing follows the arc length. Multi-line text is bent as one block.
  - **Centre arc on object** puts the arc centre on the object's centre. With a round flat face under the text, Radius mode starts prefilled from that face. While the section is open, the reference circle and its centre are drawn in the 3D view.
  - **With Use surface**, letters are placed one by one on the surface (**Projection: Per letter**, the default), each with its own position and orientation, so a sphere, a domed lid rim or the wall of a cup keeps even letter spacing and no letter is stretched or clipped. **Parallel** projection is still available. On a cylinder wall the arc is the exact development of the flat arc.
  - Curve is not available together with the old Per glyph mode while it is on. Curve settings are saved in the 3MF and work with undo and redo; old files open unchanged and old builds keep the baked mesh.
- **Shapes and symbols inside text** (#292, #294). The text box has an **Insert** popup with three tabs:
  - **Shapes**: 25 built-in shapes.
  - **Symbols**: 122 common symbols in groups (stars, check marks, arrows and so on). A bundled symbol font (a Noto Sans Symbols 2 subset) also fills in any character the chosen font lacks, so these show up in the text box and in the 3D text instead of an empty space or a `?`.
  - **My SVGs**: **From SVG file...** adds your own SVG as a one-colour silhouette. The tab also lists the SVGs already used in the project. The path of the file is not stored.
  - A shape counts as one character in the text box (backspace, selection, copy and paste), sits on the baseline at cap height and follows bold and italic. Shapes work with Curve, Use surface and per-letter placement, so a star can follow an arc around a cup.
  - Saved in the 3MF (user SVGs as `3D/inline_*.svg` entries, shared when several texts use the same one). Older builds open the file and keep the baked mesh, but editing the text there drops the shapes.
  - Limits: 32 shapes and 200 placeholder characters per text. There is no per-shape size or flip control yet.
- **Print history per plate** (#297). A history icon (clock with a check badge) at the bottom of each plate's icon column opens a list, newest first, of when, where and on which machine the plate was sent. The icon turns green once the plate went to a printer.
  - Recorded on success only, from: Bambu print and send-to-storage jobs (LAN and cloud), multi-printer send, the print-host queue (OctoPrint, Moonraker, PrusaLink, FlashForge, Duet, Elegoo and others), the Snapmaker pre-print page, phone sends, and exports of G-code and sliced plates. Exports are listed as "Exported to file" but never turn the icon green.
  - A plate that changed after its last send shows a "modified since last print" note, and the icon stays green. Each entry carries the plate number, plate name and project title.
  - **Clear history** asks first. Up to 50 entries per plate are saved with the project (not in Bambu exports) and older builds ignore them. A print that happened is not rolled back by undo.
- **Nozzle-only zones on H2D, H2C and X2D** (#293). The strips of the plate that only one nozzle reaches are shaded and hatched, with "left nozzle only" / "right nozzle only" labels, in Prepare and Preview and in light and dark themes (the H2C's 5 mm right strip is shaded without a label).
  - **Reach check at slice time.** Slicing now stops with an error when a filament assigned to one nozzle is used by an object that nozzle cannot reach, by footprint or by height. The message names the filament, the nozzle, the objects and the nozzle's X/Y/Z range. In automatic mode it fails only when no nozzle can print every object of a filament. A prime tower outside the shared area, with both nozzles purging into it, gives a warning instead.
  - Automatic filament grouping now keeps a filament off a nozzle that would have to leave its reach.
  - Single-nozzle printers, the four-toolhead U1 and the FlashForge Creator 5 are unaffected.
  - This needed a profile loader fix: the left and right nozzle areas of every H2D, H2C and X2D profile were being merged into one shape. Projects saved by older builds are repaired on load.
- **FlashForge Device tab** (#309). The tab now lists only FlashForge printers, taken from your print-host settings and saved printers.
  - **Add printer:** serial number, IP address and check code (or pick an existing Flashforge physical printer, or search the network). It is checked with a read-only call, saved with its address and connected at once.
  - Saved printers reconnect when the tab opens, and a refused check code no longer deletes the saved printer.
  - Saved Bambu LAN printers no longer show up as Offline tiles in this tab, and Bambu's device list in turn ignores FlashForge entries.
  - Warning-level `[FlashForge]` lines in the log cover FlashNetwork start-up, saved printers, connect and Add printer.

## Behaviour changes

- **No startup contact with Snapmaker or Bambu unless you use them** (#291).
  - Snapmaker: the hidden sign-in check at id.snapmaker.com runs only if you have signed in to Snapmaker on this computer, and a sign-out request with no token no longer goes out. **After updating, if you were signed in to Snapmaker, sign in once more by hand**; with a valid web session the sign-in window closes by itself. Snapmaker U1 over the LAN is unaffected.
  - Bambu: the startup sync of Bambu printer data and the plug-in update check now run only when a Bambu printer is set up (a Bambu printer preset, a Bambu printer in the Device tab, found by LAN discovery or saved, or a Bambu cloud login). Adding a printer or signing in starts it without a restart. It never runs in Stealth mode, which the old sync ignored. While UltraNet is installed the plug-in check is skipped.
- **A new pairing link drops old phones** (#291). Creating a new link in the tray or on the hub page now also removes every registered phone's push registration (and browser push subscriptions), and the confirmation says phones stop getting notifications. Pair again to receive them. Removing a single phone works as before.
- **Opaque notification identifiers** (#291). Push notifications no longer carry the printer's serial number in their grouping and collapse identifiers; they use per-hub keyed ids instead. The printer id stays inside the encrypted payload. Current phone app builds keep working. Notifications already on a lock screen won't collapse with newer ones, once.
- **Crash reports no longer include the computer name** (#296). The report's server name is replaced with `redacted`, and the computer-name environment variables (COMPUTERNAME, USERDOMAIN, LOGONSERVER, CLIENTNAME and so on) are overwritten before a report is made. Crash reporting stays opt-in. Local, test and agent builds now report as `development` with a `-dev+<commit>` version, so they no longer mix with release crashes.
- **The H2D, H2C and X2D reach error can block a slice that used to be allowed** (#293). A plate that put an object where its assigned nozzle can't reach used to slice (and print badly or fail on the printer). It is now refused with an error that names the object and the nozzle. Move the object, assign the filament to the other nozzle, or let automatic grouping choose.
- **Per-letter is the default for curved text on a surface** (#289). Turning on Curve with Use surface switches the projection to Per letter. Choose Parallel in the Curve section to get the single-projection look.

## Fixes

- **Prime tower on the wrong plate** (#288). After an undo or redo, two plates could end up sharing one print, so plate 1 sliced with its prime tower drawn on plate 2's spot and the slice failed ("Failed 0/2"). Undo and redo no longer reset the plate list's print slots, and a plate that finds its slot taken gets a print of its own.
- **Curved text crash** (#295). Dragging the Smile or Arch angle to a small value with Use surface on could crash the app. Local curvature is now measured over a longer stretch and letters stay straight when it can't be trusted, warped letters are cleaned before the cut, and any failure in a text update now shows a warning ("The text could not be updated... The previous shape is kept.") instead of reaching the main loop.
- **Text on a surface crash** (#289). Text whose glyph side passes exactly through a mesh vertex (for example box letters centred on a sphere) crashed the surface cut. Those faces are now classified correctly.
- **HMS panel crash** (#296). Clicking a row while a printer update rebuilt the list could use a freed row. The browser launch is now queued and the handler works on copies.
- **SVG size limits** (#290). The SVG tool and imported .svg files refuse files over 8 MiB or with too many shapes, paths or points, with a message. SVGs embedded in 3MF files are read through a capped reader; a refused entry keeps the baked mesh and only disables re-editing the SVG.
- **Small binary STL files** (#299, OrcaSlicer #16130). Binary STLs with zero normals and low coordinates, such as a 10 mm or 15 mm cube, were read as text, gave 0 facets and failed to load. The file size is now checked against the facet count.
- **Gap-fill memory leak** (#298, OrcaSlicer #16137). Every solid surface with gap fill leaked its gap-fill paths on each slice. The G-code is byte-identical.
- **Dual-nozzle confirmation lost on load** (#297). The per-plate dual-nozzle confirmation was saved in the project but not restored when it was opened. It is now.
- **Check Windows Firewall** (#309). On a Public network, with the rules already in place, the Fix button used to loop. It is now disabled and the dialog says to switch the network to Private (Public is never opened silently). The check and the installer also add a rule, **EdgeSlicer FlashForge discovery** (UDP 18007, Private and Domain), so FlashForge printers are found. The installer and uninstaller handle the rule too.
- **H2D, H2C and X2D position pickers.** The timelapse position picker and the prime tower run-in clamp were reading the merged nozzle area (see New) and now use the real per-nozzle areas (#293).
- **UltraNet plug-in overwrite guard** (#291). A stale Bambu network plug-in staged in the update folder could be copied over UltraNet's `bambu_networking.dll` at startup. That copy is now refused while UltraNet is installed (unless you chose to keep a foreign plug-in), the staged files are removed by exact name and a warning is logged.

## Under the hood and safety

- **Privacy audit follow-up** (#291). The startup Bambu sync is a separate, once-per-session step with its own policy (`BambuSyncPolicy`), the Snapmaker sign-in marker is stored in the app config, and a hosted-push queue entry is dropped when its device is removed. `docs/privacy.md` is updated.
- **Push identifiers** (#291). A per-hub key (32 random bytes in `settings.json`, never in any response) feeds HMAC-based thread and collapse ids for APNs, FCM, the hosted service and Web Push.
- **Bounded SVG reading** (#290, #294). SVG entries declare their size before anything is allocated. Inline-shape entries in a 3MF must match the writer's exact name form, go through the archive path normaliser and the same cap, and bad entries or hostile JSON come out empty without a crash.
- **Plate print slots** (#288). New `PlatePrintSlots` helper for handing out free print indices, with headless tests that failed on the old code.
- **Print history storage** (#297). The history lives in the plate list keyed by a per-plate id, written to `model_settings.config` only when non-empty.
- **Tests:** new cases for the bend, per-letter placement on a sphere and cup wall (with an on-demand sweep of small angles and radii), inline text and shapes, the extruder areas and reach check on the real H2D profile, plate print slots, print history, push privacy, the Snapmaker sign-in marker, the Bambu sync policy, the plug-in guard, FlashForge saved printers and firewall rules, and small binary STL detection.
